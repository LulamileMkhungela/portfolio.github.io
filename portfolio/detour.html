Why Detour

Off the usual path!

Every Detour trip starts with someone who actually lives there: a guesthouse host in Cape Town, a guide who grew up in the Berg, a skipper out of Stone Town. We build each route around them, so you see more than the postcard stops.

Local hosts and communities shape what is on the plan, and your flight and stay arrive in one booking. Expect a braai on the stoep instead of a tour bus, and a lot fewer open tabs.
