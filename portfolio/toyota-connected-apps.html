Toyota Connected Apps

One owner, three doors.

Read the story
ClientToyota South Africa, via iOCO
RoleSenior UI/UX Designer & Ionic/Angular Developer
IndustryAutomotive
Year2022 — 2024

Toyota's ownership experience used to live in call centres and dealer desks. Across two apps — the owner companion (today's MyToyota) and Toyota Remote — I was the senior designer and one of the developers: I designed the owner journeys in Figma and built them in Ionic and Angular, on the same design system as the rest of the Toyota programme, Lexus included. This one case study covers both products; the live apps are one tap below.

I delivered UI design in Figma and functional product in Ionic/Angular. Across the wider Toyota engagement I also built a structured UX research practice with Hotjar across 10+ African markets, turning behavioural data into a prioritised design backlog tied to Toyota's KPIs.

What I did

Owner journeys, remote states and one design system.

02

Connected-car UX

Toyota Remote's lock, climate, status and tracking flows, designed around state and feedback: what the car did, when, and what is still pending.

Service booking
