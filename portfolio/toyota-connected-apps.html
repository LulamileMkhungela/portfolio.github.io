Toyota Connected Apps

Ownership, from call centres to your phone.

ClientToyota South Africa · via iOCO
RoleDevsigner: full ownership of UX/UI design and frontend
IndustryAutomotive
Year2022 — 2024

Toyota's ownership experience lived in call centres and dealer desks. Across two apps, the owner companion (today's MyToyota) and Toyota Remote, I designed the owner journeys in Figma and built them in Ionic and Angular, on the same design system as the rest of the Toyota programme.

The problem

Ownership admin was split across channels

01Ownership admin was split across channelsServicing, bookings, finance and support each needed a different call or desk.
02Remote commands did not say what happenedA connected-car command has to read as done, pending or failed. Without that, people cannot trust it.
03Booking needed a phone callAppointments depended on contacting a dealer rather than seeing real availability.

My role and boundaries

I owned UX/UI design and frontend for both apps: the owner journeys, the connected-car states, the booking flow and the component layer. I designed in Figma and built in Ionic and Angular, so the design and the shipped build came from one person.

Key decisions

01

One companion app for ownership admin

Servicing, bookings, finance and support sit in the same app.

02

Design remote commands around state and feedback

The journey shows what the car did, when, and what is still pending, so a lock command reads as done, pending or failed.

03

Book against real dealer availability

Appointments are made without a phone call, using the dealer's actual slots.

04

Use one design system across the Toyota programme

The same tokens as the brand programme and Lexus, so one identity runs across every surface.

Outcome and evidence

Figures are shown only where they were published. Other results are described qualitatively.

01One place for ownership adminQualitative: servicing, bookings, finance and support in one app.
02Remote buttons with clear stateQualitative: lock, climate and status commands show done, pending or failed.
03Service booking without a phone callQualitative: appointments are made against real dealer availability.
04Behavioural research across 10+ African marketsHotjar research turned into a prioritised design backlog tied to Toyota's KPIs. No metrics are published here.
