Toyota Connected Apps

Toyota's ownership experience used to live in call centres and dealer desks. I designed and built the owner app and the remote-control app, on one design system shared with the rest of the Toyota programme.

RoleSenior UI/UX designer and Ionic/Angular developer
DatesAug 2023 – Apr 2025
EmployeriOCO, for Toyota South Africa
ScopeFigma design, Ionic/Angular build, UX research

Two apps, one owner

Owner companion (MyToyota)
Servicing, bookings, finance and support in one place. The Toyota App evolved into today's MyToyota.
Toyota Remote
Lock, climate, vehicle status and tracking, designed around state and feedback.

I designed the owner journeys in Figma and built them in Ionic and Angular.

Research

I built a structured UX research practice with Hotjar across 10+ African markets, turning behavioural data into a prioritised design backlog tied to Toyota's KPIs.

Decisions

1. Show what a remote command actually did

A lock, climate or tracking command reads as done, pending or failed, and the app shows what the car did, when, and what is still pending. Owners can trust the button.

2. Book servicing against real dealer availability

Appointments are made against real dealer availability, without a phone call.

3. One design system across the brand

The same tokens as KINTO, the brand programme and Lexus, so one identity runs across every surface.

Across Toyota, KINTO and Lexus

MyToyota carries the owner companion forward, Toyota Remote handles connected-car controls, and the Lexus South Africa app is the connected ownership companion for Lexus owners, built on the same system.
